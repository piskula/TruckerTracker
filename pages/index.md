# TruckTrack Support

TruckTrack helps fleet companies track vehicle issues from the moment a driver reports them until a mechanic fixes them.

## Contact

Email **[momosi.labs@gmail.com](mailto:momosi.labs@gmail.com)**. We answer within a few working days.

## Common questions

**How do I get an account?**
Accounts are created by your company's administrator. It is not possible to sign up yourself.

**I forgot my password or can't sign in.**
Contact your company's administrator, or email us.

**How do I delete my account?**
Ask your company's administrator, or email us. Account details are removed within 30 days. See the [privacy policy](privacy/) for details.

**The app crashed or something doesn't work.**
Email us with your phone model, the app version (Profile screen) and what you were doing.
