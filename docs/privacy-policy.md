# TruckTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## Who runs TruckTrack

TruckTrack is built and run by Momosi, a small group of independent developers. Momosi is responsible for the personal data the app handles. Contact: momosi.labs@gmail.com.

## What the app collects

- **Account details**: name, username and email, used for signing in.
- **What users enter**: vehicles, issues, comments, attached photos and files, and the history of changes. Other users in the same company can see these, together with the author's name.
- **Crash reports**: if the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends the crash details, device model, system version and app version.
- **Server logs**: IP address and request times, kept for security and fixing problems.

The app does not collect location or contacts, has no ads, and does not track users. Momosi never sells personal data.

## Why the data is used

Only to run the app, fix crashes and keep the service secure.

## Who else gets it

- **Google Firebase**, for crash reports. Google may process this data in the USA under EU-approved safeguards ([Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)).
- **Our hosting provider**, which runs the server and stores the data on behalf of Momosi. It may use the data only to provide that service.

No one else, unless the law requires it.

## How long it is kept

- Account details: until the account is deleted.
- Crash reports: 90 days.
- Server logs: 30 days.

## Accounts and account deletion

It is not possible to register in TruckTrack yourself. Accounts are created by the administrator of the company that uses TruckTrack.

To delete an account, contact your company's administrator, or email momosi.labs@gmail.com. Account details are removed within 30 days. Issues and comments the user created stay available to the rest of the company, shown as "Deleted user".

## Your rights

You can ask Momosi to show, correct, export or delete your data, or to stop using it. Email momosi.labs@gmail.com; requests are answered within one month. You can also complain to the [Slovak Office for Personal Data Protection](https://dataprotection.gov.sk).

## Security

All data travels encrypted over HTTPS. Sign-in happens on a secure login page, so the app never stores passwords.

## Children

TruckTrack is a work tool for adults and is not meant for children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and bigger changes are announced in the app.
